Samsung S25: Features and Upgrades from S24

Design and Build

The Samsung S25 introduces a refined design with a sleeker build compared to its predecessor, the S24. The phone boasts a lighter frame and an improved edge-to-edge display for a more immersive experience.

The use of recycled materials in the S25 aligns with Samsung's commitment to sustainability while maintaining premium quality.

Display Improvements

The S25 features a 6.9-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with a higher peak brightness of 2200 nits, making it perfect for outdoor use. This is a significant improvement from the S24, which had a peak brightness of 1750 nits.

With a 120Hz adaptive refresh rate and improved color accuracy, the S25 delivers smoother scrolling and vibrant visuals.

Performance Upgrades

Powered by the latest Exynos 2500 (or Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 in select markets), the S25 offers a 20% boost in CPU performance and 30% in GPU efficiency. This makes multitasking and gaming faster and smoother.

The S25 is also equipped with up to 16GB of LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 4.1 storage, ensuring faster app loading and seamless performance.

Camera Enhancements

Photography enthusiasts will love the upgraded 200MP main sensor, which offers better low-light performance and richer details compared to the S24's 108MP sensor.

The S25 also includes improved AI image processing, a 50MP telephoto lens with 5x optical zoom, and an ultra-wide lens for capturing breathtaking landscapes.

Battery Life and Charging

The S25 comes with a larger 5000mAh battery, optimized with AI-based power management for extended usage. Compared to the S24, the S25 promises 10% longer battery life.

Fast charging gets an upgrade with 65W wired charging and 30W wireless charging support, allowing you to power up in less time.

Software and Connectivity

The Samsung S25 ships with One UI 6.1, based on Android 14, offering a cleaner, more customizable user experience. Features like improved multitasking, widgets, and privacy settings make it stand out.

In terms of connectivity, the S25 supports Wi-Fi 7, enhanced 5G speeds, and satellite communication for emergencies, a feature absent in the S24.

Price and Availability

The Samsung S25 starts at $1,099, which is a slight increase from the S24's starting price. Pre-orders begin on February 15, 2025, with availability in stores by March 1, 2025.
